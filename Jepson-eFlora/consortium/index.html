CCH Data Portals

   The Consortium of California Herbaria (CCH) is the organization that supports all herbaria in California. Established in 2003, the Consortium now includes records from over 70 California herbaria. For more information about the CCH, see the about page (https://ucjeps.berkeley.edu/consortium/about.html).
CCH manages two data portals and each has a special purpose.

CCH1



Searching CCH1 is currently disabled while the site is under re-development. The CCH1 database is composed of augmented data from California vascular plant specimens housed in herbaria, worldwide. There are two main data portal from which California data was harvested:

  • Specimens from around 200 worldwide herbaria can be found in GBIF.
  • Specimens from around 70 California herbaria can be found in CCH2.

CCH2



Consortium of California Herbaria Portal 2 serves data from all specimens (nonvascular and vascular) housed in CCH member herbaria.
